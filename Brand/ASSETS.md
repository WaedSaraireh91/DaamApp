# Daam visual identity

Created using the built-in image-generation tool. The app uses the following project-local assets in `DaamApp/Resources/Assets.xcassets`:

- `DaamLogo.imageset/DaamLogo.png`: refined teal open-book/supportive-hands emblem with a gold rising star. A standalone copy is `Brand/DaamLogo.png`; the earlier concept is retained as `Brand/DaamLogo-v1.png`.
- `LearningArt.imageset/LearningArt.png`: premium 3D clay illustration of a tutor and schoolgirl learning at a desk; teal, mint, ivory and gold, toy-like fictional characters, no realistic people, no text.
- `TeachingArt.imageset/TeachingArt.png`: friendly 3D animated-style male tutor with notebook, pencil, books and floating star; warm ivory background and teal/gold palette, no photographic people.
- `ScheduleArt.imageset/ScheduleArt.png`: rounded clay calendar, clock, books, pencil and golden check; ivory backdrop, teal/gold palette, no words or numbers.

## Final logo prompt

Design a polished innovative standalone emblem for Daam, a warm premium Arabic tutoring app. A bold minimal symmetrical open book transforms into two supportive hands around one small golden rising star of possibility, intelligent negative space, elegant rounded geometry, just 3 or 4 strong shapes, recognizable at tiny app icon size. Deep teal #0E736E book/hands, golden #F2AB30 star. Truly flat vector-like solid color, crisp edges, no gradients, no texture, no shadows, no typography, no letters, no mockup. Single centered mark occupying 70 percent of a square canvas with a transparent background. Understated, friendly, timeless, professionally designed identity.

The scene descriptions above document the final prompt set. Generated images are raster assets; no vector-master claim is made. Motion is implemented separately in SwiftUI.
